Three generations of Cork craftsmanship

Established in 1988, Grange Ironcraft is a family-run business based in Fermoy, Co. Cork, specialising in custom metal fabrication for residential, retail and commercial requirements.

From one-of-a-kind pieces to complex structural solutions, our team combines innovation, precision, and expertise to create products that are built to last and designed to impress.
